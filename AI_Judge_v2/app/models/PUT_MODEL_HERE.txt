export_for_cpu.py (run after training) writes these files into this folder:
  judge-v2-q8_0.gguf     the trained judge (about 4.3 GB)
  Modelfile              tells Ollama how to load it
  prompt_format.json     the exact prompt wrapper used in training
  MODEL_INFO.txt         version label
Then:  ollama create judge-v2 -f Modelfile     (run inside this folder)
